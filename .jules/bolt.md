## 2024-06-14 - Migrating Framer Motion img tags to next/image
**Learning:** When migrating from `<motion.img>` to Next.js `<Image>`, you cannot use `<motion.Image>` directly with the Next.js component. Additionally, when porting over custom `onError` fallback handling, browsers will prioritize the `srcset` attribute automatically generated by Next.js `next/image` over the `src` attribute fallback, causing the image to remain broken.
**Action:** Always wrap the `next/image` in a `<motion.div>` rather than trying to animate the Next component directly, and when specifying an `onError` handler, be sure to clear `e.target.srcset = ""` so the fallback `src` triggers correctly.
